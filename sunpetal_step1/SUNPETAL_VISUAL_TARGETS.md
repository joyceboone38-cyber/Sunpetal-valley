# Sunpetal Valley — Visual Targets for Step 1

## Core Visual Keywords
- luminous
- magical
- underwater
- glamorous
- stylish
- pearlescent
- decorative
- dreamy
- readable
- inviting

## Palette Direction
Primary tones:
- aqua
- turquoise
- seafoam
- lavender
- lilac
- pastel pink
- pearl white

Accent tones:
- soft gold
- rose gold
- glowing cyan
- magical violet
- selective hot pink

## Surface Language
Good:
- shell sheen
- crystal glow
- subtle shimmer
- satin-like fabric feel
- glossy accents
- translucent magic details

Bad:
- chalky matte surfaces everywhere
- identical roughness values
- dull block colors
- generic low-effort clay look

## Composition Goals
- elegant curves
- shell/scallop/arch motifs
- layered detail
- focal-point lighting
- open readable center + decorated edges
- enough density to feel rich, not cluttered
