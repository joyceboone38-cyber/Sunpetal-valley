# Sunpetal Valley environment art

Use the Homebase Overview on the concept board for composition and the illustrated reef in the project for the palette. Capture a new asset in the playable 3D camera before treating it as representative of the art direction.

| Element | Visual target |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture | Wide fluted opal canopies, slender brass ribs, rounded silhouettes and warm arched windows. Match the pavilion proportions in the cottage. |
| Stone and paths | Warm worn sandstone at eye level, darker blue-violet strata along the shore and mottled variation across broad surfaces. |
| Water and depth | Cobalt depth, turquoise in the channel, irregular caustics and distant light shafts. Reserve bright glow for points of interest. |
| Scene composition | Place one readable silhouette above a warm focal area. Layer the shoreline and distant reef so the player can read the route from the swim camera. |
| Fashion reference | Glossy Y2K accessories, opalescent finishes and punchy accents. The character uses legs for levels 1–10 in the intended progression. |

Review assets in an in-game frame beside the approved reference. Check the silhouette, material response, spacing, and readability at the normal camera distance. The homebase is the first reference scene for future environments.

For open-water traversal, use a lower camera and a layered view into the reef. Let the current read as moving light, with enough open blue around it to read Luma's silhouette. Luma's motion comes from bell contractions and trailing fins. Use creature animation and depth as the connection to the Aniimo reference, with Sunpetal's own silhouettes and opal palette.
